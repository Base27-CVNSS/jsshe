About the journal

A rigorous, open forum for interdisciplinary scholarship.

Founded in 2011, UED Journal of Social Sciences, Humanities and Education is a peer-reviewed, open-access research journal published by The University of Danang – University of Science and Education.

The journal publishes original articles, source publications, reviews and other forms of scholarly expression in English and Vietnamese. Its scope covers literature and linguistics, history and archaeology, philosophy and political science, cultural and religious studies, psychology, sociology and education.

The journal is included among the refereed academic journals recognized by Vietnam’s State Council for Professorship for relevant disciplines.

Editorial standards

A clear path from submission to publication

  1. 01

    Submission

    Scope, originality and basic requirements are checked by the editorial office.

  2. 02

    Editorial screening

    An editor assesses scholarly fit, integrity and readiness for external review.

  3. 03

    Double-anonymous review

    Independent specialists evaluate the manuscript without author or reviewer identities.

  4. 04

    Decision & revision

    Authors address reviewer comments; editors make and document the final decision.

  5. 05

    Production

    Accepted work is copyedited, typeset, assigned metadata and published online.
