  1. 01

    Socialization in the Preservation of Urban Traditional Culture: International Experiences and Recommendations for Vietnam (Case Studies of Hoi An and Hue)

    Vo Huu Hoa

    DOI 10.47393/jshe.vol15.i2.1180 ↗

    Abstract

    This article examines how broad social participation can support the preservation of traditional culture in historic cities. Drawing on international experience and the cases of Hoi An and Hue, it considers the roles of public authorities, local communities, cultural practitioners, and private organizations, then outlines recommendations for more coordinated and sustainable heritage governance in Vietnam.

    Keywords

    cultural heritage · community participation · urban conservation · Hoi An · Hue

  2. 02

    Developing Measures to Improve Mental Health Help-Seeking Behaviors Among Secondary School Students

    Nguyen Le Bao Hoang · Huynh Van Son · Bui Hong Quan

    DOI 10.47393/jshe.vol15.i2.3797 ↗

    Abstract

    The study develops practical measures for strengthening mental health help-seeking among secondary school students. It considers barriers such as limited awareness, stigma, trust, and access to support, and frames help-seeking as a shared responsibility across students, schools, families, and counselling services. The proposed measures emphasize early recognition, approachable support channels, and a safer school climate.

    Keywords

    mental health · help-seeking behavior · secondary students · school counselling · stigma

  3. 03

    From “Underlying Narrative Strand” to “Reflection”: The Narrative Artistry in The Scholars by Wu Jingzi

    Le Sy Dien

    DOI 10.47393/jshe.vol15.i2.3664 ↗

    Abstract

    This literary study reads Wu Jingzi’s The Scholars through the movement from an underlying narrative strand to reflective narration. It examines how plot organization, recurring characters, and shifts in narrative attention create coherence across an episodic novel. The analysis shows how these techniques sustain satire while turning individual stories into a broader reflection on scholars and social life.

    Keywords

    The Scholars · Wu Jingzi · narrative art · reflection · satire

  4. 04

    Common Dimensions of Gender Prejudice in Gender-Biased Utterances (Through Vietnamese Literary Corpora)

    Tran Thi Mai Huong

    DOI 10.47393/jshe.vol15.i2.3661 ↗

    Abstract

    Using a Vietnamese literary corpus, this article identifies recurring dimensions of prejudice in utterances directed toward women and men. The analysis highlights how judgements about personality and family roles remain especially prominent across gendered speech. It demonstrates how literary language can preserve and circulate social expectations, offering a linguistic basis for recognizing patterns of gender bias.

    Keywords

    gender prejudice · biased utterances · Vietnamese literature · corpus analysis · family roles

  5. 05

    Compliments in Asynchronous Online Feedback: Implications in L2 Writing Classrooms

    Thu M. Lau

    DOI 10.47393/jshe.vol15.i2.3680 ↗

    Abstract

    The paper examines the act of giving compliments in asynchronous online feedback from writing tutors. It considers how positive evaluation functions alongside corrective commentary and discusses the implications for second-language writing classrooms. The study presents compliments as a pragmatic resource that can support rapport, learner confidence, and engagement when used with clear, substantive guidance.

    Keywords

    compliments · asynchronous feedback · L2 writing · writing tutors · pragmatics

  6. 06

    The Relationship Between Stress, Anxiety, Depression and Fear of COVID-19 Among Students at Dalat College

    Nguyen Thanh Truc · Nguyen Thi Nhu Thuy

    DOI 10.47393/jshe.vol15.i2.3660 ↗

    Abstract

    This study investigates the relationship between fear of COVID-19 and symptoms of stress, anxiety, and depression among students at Dalat College. By considering these experiences together, it clarifies how pandemic-related fear may accompany broader psychological distress in a student population. The findings provide a basis for targeted screening, prevention, and campus mental health support.

    Keywords

    stress · anxiety · depression · fear of COVID-19 · college students

  7. 07

    Modality Markers in Vietnamese Sentence Fragments

    Trinh Quynh Dong Nghi

    Original article View on JSHE ↗

    Abstract

    The article identifies modality markers in Vietnamese sentence fragments and uses them to explain the status and rhetorical effect of fragmentary constructions. It considers how linguistic forms that fall outside a complete sentence can still convey speaker stance, evaluation, and pragmatic meaning. The analysis connects grammatical description with the expressive force of fragments in context.

    Keywords

    modality · modality markers · sentence fragments · Vietnamese · rhetoric

  8. 08

    A Survey of English–Vietnamese Translation Errors by Google Translate in Terms of Connotational Meaning of Collocation in The Greatness Guide

    Le Tan Thi · Pham Thi Tuyet Ngan

    Original article View on JSHE ↗

    Abstract

    This survey examines errors in Google Translate’s English–Vietnamese rendering of collocations with connotational meaning in The Greatness Guide. It focuses on cases where a literal or context-insensitive translation weakens the intended associations of a phrase. The analysis illustrates the limits of automated translation when lexical combinations depend on tone, cultural knowledge, and contextual interpretation.

    Keywords

    Google Translate · collocation · connotational meaning · English–Vietnamese translation · translation errors

  9. 09

    Bottom-Up Development: Living Heritage and Identity Strategies Among Ethnic Minority Youth

    Phan Thuy Giang

    DOI 10.47393/jshe.vol15.i2.3751 ↗

    Abstract

    Drawing on the concepts of living heritage and bottom-up development, this article examines how ethnic minority youth negotiate cultural identity through everyday heritage practices. It foregrounds young people as active participants rather than passive recipients of preservation policy. Their strategies connect cultural continuity with contemporary aspirations and show how community-led action can shape more locally grounded development.

    Keywords

    living heritage · bottom-up development · ethnic minority youth · identity · community agency

  10. 10

    Buddhist Economy Under Rama V: State Patronage Policy and Its Impact on the Sangha

    Pham Quoc Trung · Syngernmanh Khamphamay

    Original article View on JSHE ↗

    Abstract

    This historical analysis considers the Buddhist economy under Chulalongkorn (Rama V, 1868–1910), when Buddhism served both as a spiritual foundation and as part of state consolidation. It examines how royal patronage affected the Sangha’s organization, resources, and relationship with political authority. The study places monastic change within Thailand’s wider projects of centralization and modernization.

    Keywords

    Buddhist economy · Rama V · state patronage · Sangha · Thailand
